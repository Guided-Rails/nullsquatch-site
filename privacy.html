Privacy policy

Null Squatch is in development and has not been publicly released. This page describes what the app will collect once it is available, and will be expanded before public release.

The app will collect:

  • Location traces. While you record a walk, the app logs your route so it can mark the ground you covered on the map. Traces are uploaded to our servers when the walk ends.
  • Apple sign-in identifier. If you sign in with Apple, we store the identifier Apple gives us so we can keep your coverage and stats tied to your account.

We do not sell your data and we do not run third-party analytics or advertising.
